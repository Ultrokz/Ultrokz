🏗️ Development Philosophy

«Build. Break. Learn. Improve. Repeat.»

I believe experimentation is one of the fastest ways to understand technology.

Projects may begin as small experiments and eventually evolve into:

- Experiment
    ↓
- Prototype
    ↓
- Tool
    ↓
- Framework
    ↓
- Open Source Project

Not every experiment becomes a finished project — and that's part of development.
